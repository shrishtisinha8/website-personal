Analytics & risk modelling

Wells Fargo

Data Analyst InternMay – July 2023

Implemented Pluto–Tasche and logistic-regression approaches to estimate probability of default in low-default portfolios, using Python, Excel and R.

Approach: estimating default risk with sparse data+

Problem

Few or no observed defaults can produce zero or near-zero default-rate estimates that understate uncertainty.

Estimation approach

  • Applied Pluto–Tasche’s most prudent estimation principle: estimate default probabilities using upper confidence bounds while preserving the ordering of rating grades.
  • Used this upper-bound approach for conservative estimation when default observations were scarce, alongside logistic regression as a comparison methodology.
Method reference: Pluto & Tasche — Estimating Probabilities of Default for Low Default Portfolios

Biodiversity research

University College London

Visiting Researcher · Final-year master’s thesisAugust 2023 – June 2024

Modelled the impacts of climate change and land use on biodiversity for my final-year master’s thesis with the Centre for Biodiversity and Environment Research.

Ran climate-only, land-use-only and combined-change simulations for Lasioglossum albipes across Europe, setting model parameters and coefficients and comparing projected species occurrence.

Research question & simulation approach+

Research question

How might climate change and land-use change, separately and together, affect the occurrence of the focal species across Europe?

My contribution

  1. Configured climate and land-use parameters and coefficients for the modelling workflow.
  2. Loaded those inputs into the model and ran climate-only, land-use-only and combined-driver simulations.
  3. Compared the resulting occurrence projections to explore how the environmental scenarios changed the modelled species patterns.

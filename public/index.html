02Biodiversity research+

University College London · August 2023 – June 2024

Visiting Researcher, CBER Group

Undertook a visiting master’s research project on the separate and combined effects of climate and land-use change on insect biodiversity.

Configured and ran scenario simulations for Lasioglossum albipes across Europe, comparing projected species occurrence under climate-only, land-use-only and combined changes.

Research question & simulation approach+

Research question

How might climate change and land-use change, separately and together, affect the occurrence of the focal species across Europe?

My contribution

  1. Configured climate and land-use parameters and coefficients for the modelling workflow.
  2. Loaded those inputs into the model and ran climate-only, land-use-only and combined-driver simulations.
  3. Compared the resulting occurrence projections to explore how the environmental scenarios changed the modelled species patterns.

The work focused on simulation setup, model runs and scenario comparison as part of the group’s broader biodiversity-modelling research.
